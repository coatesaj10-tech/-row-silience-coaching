1.1. Bookings must be made through the official Row-silience registration process.

1.6. Participants must contact Row-silience before booking if they are unsure whether the camp is suitable for their experience, ability or circumstances.

3.1. If a participant wishes to cancel their booking, they must notify Row-silience in writing using coaches@row-silience.co.uk.

3.2. We ask participants to provide as much notice as possible if they can no longer attend.

3.3. Where a participant cancels, any refund will be considered in accordance with the following policy:
