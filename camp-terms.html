Booking Terms & Conditions

Last updated: November 2026

These Terms & Conditions apply to all bookings for rowing camps organised by Row-silience, an ordinary partnership operated by Holly Dunford and Alexander Coates (AJ).

By submitting a booking form, you confirm that you have read, understood and agreed to these Terms & Conditions.

1. Booking and eligibility

1.1. Bookings must be made through the official Row-silience booking form.

1.2. Places are limited and allocated on a first-come, first-served basis, subject to availability and subsequent payment.

1.3. Submitting the booking form reserves a provisional place. It does not require immediate payment or guarantee a fully confirmed place.

1.4. A booking is only fully confirmed once the minimum participant requirement has been met, Row-silience has confirmed that the camp will proceed, full payment has been received by bank transfer and Row-silience has acknowledged receipt.

1.5. The November camp is an open-level Masters camp for participants aged 18 or over, welcoming rowers of all experience levels. We do not accept junior participants.

1.6. Participants must provide accurate and complete information during registration, including relevant rowing experience, emergency contact details and any medical or other information that may affect their safe participation.

1.7. Participants must contact Row-silience before booking if they are unsure whether the camp is suitable for their experience, ability or circumstances.

2. Minimum participant numbers and camp confirmation

2.1. A minimum of five participants is required for the camp to go ahead.

2.2. If fewer than five participants have submitted a booking by the registration deadline, Row-silience reserves the right to cancel the camp.

2.3. No payment will be requested or collected until at least five participants have booked and Row-silience has confirmed that the camp will proceed.

2.4. Once the minimum number has been reached, Row-silience will contact participants by email to confirm that the camp is going ahead and provide payment instructions.

2.5. Participants will be given a payment deadline and the relevant bank transfer details. Payment must be made in full by that deadline to secure their place.

2.6. If a participant does not make payment by the stated deadline, Row-silience reserves the right to release their provisional place and offer it to someone else.

2.7. If the minimum number is not reached and the camp is cancelled, participants will be notified by email. As no payment will have been collected, no refund will be necessary.

3. Payment and pricing

3.1. The advertised camp fee is £335 per person, unless a different price is clearly stated for a particular camp.

3.2. No payment is required when submitting the initial booking form.

3.3. Once the minimum participant requirement has been met and the camp is confirmed, Row-silience will email participants with instructions for paying by bank transfer.

3.4. The full camp fee must be paid by the payment deadline provided in the confirmation email.

3.5. A booking is not fully confirmed until full payment has been received and acknowledged by Row-silience.

3.6. Participants are responsible for ensuring that the bank transfer is made correctly, using the payment reference and details supplied by Row-silience.

3.7. Any additional costs not expressly included in the advertised camp price, such as travel, accommodation or personal equipment, are the responsibility of the participant.

4. Participant cancellations and refunds

4.1. If a participant wishes to cancel their booking, they must notify Row-silience in writing using coaches@row-silience.co.uk.

4.2. Participants who cancel before making payment will not be charged a cancellation fee.

4.3. We ask participants to provide as much notice as possible if they can no longer attend.

4.4. Where a participant has paid and subsequently cancels, any refund will be considered in accordance with the following policy and applicable consumer law:

  • More than 14 days before the camp: A full refund may be provided, less any clearly disclosed non-recoverable booking costs, where applicable.
  • Between 7 and 14 days before the camp: A partial refund may be provided, taking account of costs already incurred and the opportunity to fill the place.
  • Less than 7 days before the camp: Refunds will generally not be available, unless required by law or agreed at Row-silience's discretion.

4.5. Participants may request to transfer their place to another eligible adult, subject to Row-silience's prior written approval. Any replacement participant must complete the required registration and safety information before attending.

4.6. Nothing in this section affects any statutory rights that a participant may have under applicable consumer law. The statutory cancellation rules for services relating to leisure activities on a specific date or period may apply differently from those for other online purchases.

5. Cancellation or changes by Row-silience

5.1. Row-silience reserves the right to cancel, postpone or make reasonable changes to a camp where necessary.

5.2. This may include circumstances such as:

  • Fewer than five participants booking by the registration deadline.
  • Severe weather or unsafe water conditions.
  • Venue or facility closures.
  • Safety concerns.
  • Coach illness or unavailability.
  • Other circumstances outside our reasonable control.

5.3. If Row-silience cancels a camp because the minimum participant number has not been reached, participants will be notified by email. No payment will have been collected.

5.4. If Row-silience cancels a camp after payment has been collected and cannot offer a suitable alternative, participants will be offered a full refund of the camp fee.

5.5. If a camp is postponed or rescheduled, participants will be given the option to transfer their booking to the revised date or request a refund where the alternative is not suitable.

5.6. Row-silience will communicate any significant changes or cancellations as soon as reasonably possible using the contact details supplied during registration.

5.7. Where only part of a camp is affected, Row-silience will consider the circumstances and any appropriate refund or alternative arrangements.

6. Camp activities and safety

6.1. Row-silience camps may include on-water rowing, technical coaching, physical training, land-based activities and other rowing-related sessions.

6.2. Rowing and physical exercise involve inherent risks, including the possibility of injury, illness, collision, capsize or other accidents.

6.3. Participants acknowledge these risks and agree to follow all reasonable safety instructions given by Row-silience coaches, venue staff and relevant safety personnel.

6.4. Participants must:

  • Follow instructions from coaches and venue staff.
  • Behave responsibly and consider the safety of others.
  • Wear appropriate clothing and equipment.
  • Inform coaches of any injury, illness or change in health that could affect their participation.
  • Not participate while under the influence of alcohol or recreational drugs.
  • Stop an activity and inform a coach if they feel unwell, unsafe or unable to continue.

6.5. Row-silience reserves the right to restrict or remove a participant from an activity if their behaviour, health or ability to follow safety instructions creates a risk to themselves or others.

6.6. Where a participant is prevented from continuing because of a safety concern, any refund will be considered in light of the circumstances and applicable consumer rights.

6.7. Participants must not bring or use equipment that has not been approved for the relevant activity. Where participants use their own boats or equipment, they are responsible for ensuring it is suitable and safe, subject to any checks or requirements imposed by the venue or coaches.

7.2. Participants are responsible for ensuring that they are physically fit and able to take part in the activities they undertake.

7.3. Row-silience may contact a participant before the camp if further information is required to assess whether appropriate arrangements can be made.

7.4. In an emergency, participants authorise Row-silience coaches to contact emergency services and provide relevant information to assist with their care.

7.5. Participants are responsible for bringing any personal medication they may need during the camp and informing coaches of any relevant emergency requirements.
