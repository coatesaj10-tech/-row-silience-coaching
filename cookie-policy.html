1. About this policy

This Cookie Policy explains how Row-silience uses cookies and similar technologies when you visit https://row-silience.co.uk.

It should be read alongside our Privacy Policy.

2. What are cookies?

Cookies are small text files that websites may store on your computer, tablet or mobile device when you visit them.

Similar technologies, including local storage and tracking pixels, can also store or access information on your device.

These technologies may be used to support website functionality, remember preferences, understand website usage or deliver advertising.

3. Cookies and technologies used by Row-silience

Based on our review of the website pages and code currently available, Row-silience does not intentionally use:

  • Google Analytics or other website analytics tools.
  • Advertising or behavioural tracking cookies.
  • Marketing pixels or social media tracking tools.
  • Custom scripts designed to store cookies or track visitors.

Our website uses Google Fonts to display its typography. This means your browser requests font files from Google's servers. Google may receive technical information, including your IP address, as part of delivering those files. Google states that its Fonts API is designed not to create profiles of end users or use this information for advertising.

We have not identified a specific cookie set by our own website code in the pages reviewed. However, this does not guarantee that no cookies or similar technologies are used by the website hosting infrastructure or services loaded by the site.

4. Third-party services

Our website uses or links to the following third-party services:

Google Fonts

We use Google Fonts to display the fonts used throughout our website. Requests for font files are made to Google servers. For further information, see Google's Privacy Policy.

GitHub Pages

Our website is hosted using GitHub Pages. The hosting provider may process technical information when visitors access the website. For further information, see GitHub's Privacy Statement.

We use Tally to provide external enquiry and camp registration forms. When you follow a Tally link, you leave the Row-silience website and access a service operated by Tally.

Tally is responsible for the cookies and similar technologies used on its own service. Tally states that it does not use cookie tracking for advertising. You can read its Cookie Policy and Privacy Policy.

We do not directly control the technologies used by these third-party providers.

5. Managing cookies

You can manage or delete cookies through your internet browser settings. Most browsers allow you to block cookies, delete existing cookies or receive a warning before a cookie is stored.

Blocking certain cookies may affect the functionality of websites or third-party services.

Where Row-silience introduces non-essential cookies or similar technologies that require consent, we will provide clear information and obtain consent before they are used.

6. Changes to this policy

We may update this Cookie Policy if our website, hosting arrangements or third-party services change.

Any changes will be published on this page with an updated revision date.
