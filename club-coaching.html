CLUB COACHING · MASTERS PACKAGE

Bring Row-silience
to your club.

Looking to take your squad's performance to the next level? Row-silience offers tailored club coaching packages, delivered directly at your club.

We come to you to deliver practical, hands-on coaching, helping athletes improve their technique, develop as crews and get more out of their training.

You don't need the full package; on-water coaching can be booked on its own.

What's included?

  • On-water coaching sessions

    Practical coaching at your club, focusing on rowing technique, boat handling, crew coordination and overall performance.

  • Tailored training plan

    A structured training programme designed around your squad's level, goals and competition calendar.

  • Performance development

    Guidance on improving individual and crew performance, developing consistency and building effective training habits.

  • Club-specific approach

    Coaching adapted to your athletes, facilities, available equipment and club objectives.

How does it work?

We work with your club to understand your squad, current training structure and ambitions. From there, we develop a coaching package that combines on-water sessions with a tailored training plan to support continued development beyond our coaching visits.
