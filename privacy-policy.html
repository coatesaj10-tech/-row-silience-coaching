Policy

Row-silience · Last updated: 9 November 2026

1. Introduction

Row-silience is committed to protecting the privacy and personal information of everyone who visits our website, contacts us, or participates in our rowing camps, coaching sessions, training programmes, development courses and related activities.

Row-silience is an ordinary partnership operated by Holly Dunford and Alexander Coates (AJ). The partners are responsible for determining how and why personal information is collected and processed in connection with Row-silience activities.

This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, store, protect and share personal information belonging to participants, parents, guardians, coaches and other individuals involved in our programmes.

We process personal information in accordance with the UK General Data Protection Regulation (UK GDPR), the Data Protection Act 2018 and other applicable data protection legislation.

2. Information We Collect

Depending on how you interact with Row-silience, we may collect the following information:

  • Personal details: Full name, date of birth, age, gender where relevant, address, email address and telephone number.
  • Parent or guardian details: Names, contact information and relevant parental or guardian consent for participants under 18.
  • Emergency contact information: Names, telephone numbers and relationships of nominated emergency contacts.
  • Medical and health information: Relevant medical conditions, allergies, injuries, medication requirements, disabilities and other information necessary to support participant safety and welfare.
  • Rowing and sporting information: Previous rowing experience, ability level, training history, performance information and programme preferences.
  • Registration and administrative information: Enquiry details, registration information, attendance records, booking preferences and communications with participants.
  • Photographs and videos: Images or recordings taken during training sessions, camps, competitions and other organised activities, where appropriate permissions have been obtained.
  • Technical information: Limited technical information relating to visits to our website, which may be processed by our website hosting or domain providers to maintain website functionality and security.

We only collect information that is relevant and reasonably necessary for the purposes described in this policy.

3. How We Use Your Information

We use personal information for the following purposes:

  • Processing enquiries and registrations.
  • Managing bookings and organising activities.
  • Delivering rowing camps and training programmes.
  • Communicating with participants, parents and guardians.
  • Assessing rowing experience and allocating appropriate training groups.
  • Supporting participant welfare, safeguarding and health and safety.
  • Responding to medical incidents and emergencies.
  • Managing programme administration and associated records.
  • Providing updates about programme arrangements, schedules and changes.
  • Monitoring participation and improving the quality of our coaching programmes.
  • Meeting legal, regulatory, insurance and safeguarding obligations.
  • Managing complaints and resolving disputes.

We will not use personal information for purposes that are incompatible with those described in this policy without providing appropriate information and, where necessary, obtaining consent.

4. Lawful Basis for Processing Personal Information

Under the UK GDPR, we must have a lawful basis for processing personal information. Depending on the circumstances, Row-silience relies on the following lawful bases:

Contract

Where processing is necessary to take steps requested by an individual before entering into an agreement or to deliver an agreed coaching service, training programme or camp.

Legitimate Interests

Where processing is necessary for the reasonable operation and administration of Row-silience, including responding to enquiries, organising programmes, maintaining business records, improving our services and protecting our business.

We consider the impact of this processing on individuals and ensure that their rights and interests are not unfairly overridden.

Legal Obligation

Where processing is necessary to comply with applicable legal, accounting, insurance, regulatory or safeguarding requirements.

Vital Interests

Where processing is necessary to protect someone's life or prevent serious harm, particularly in an emergency where an individual is unable to provide consent.

Consent

Where we request permission for optional activities, including promotional photography, marketing communications or other optional uses of personal information.

Individuals may withdraw consent at any time where processing is based on consent.

Where we process medical information or other special-category personal data, we will also identify and rely on an appropriate additional condition under Article 9 of the UK GDPR and the Data Protection Act 2018.

Where appropriate, this may include explicit consent for processing health information. Such consent will be requested separately from general registration and participation arrangements.

5. Medical Information and Participant Welfare

The safety and welfare of our participants is a priority.

We may collect and process relevant medical and health information to:

  • Identify potential health and safety risks.
  • Make appropriate training arrangements.
  • Provide reasonable adjustments where necessary.
  • Understand relevant injuries, allergies and medical conditions.
  • Support participant welfare.
  • Respond appropriately to medical incidents and emergencies.

Medical information is treated as special-category personal data and receives additional protection under data protection legislation.

Where we rely on explicit consent to process medical information, we will request this separately and explain the purpose of collecting it.

Medical information will only be accessible to authorised individuals who need it to support participant welfare, safety or the delivery of the relevant activity.

For participants under 18, parents or legal guardians may be asked to provide accurate and up-to-date medical and emergency contact information. We will take account of the participant's age, understanding and circumstances when dealing with their information.

We will not use medical information for promotional, advertising or unrelated commercial purposes.

6. Participants Under 18

We recognise our responsibility to protect the privacy and welfare of children and young people participating in our programmes.

Where participants are under 18:

  • We may collect relevant personal information from parents or legal guardians as part of the registration process.
  • We may request appropriate parental or guardian consent where necessary.
  • We will take account of the age and understanding of the young participant.
  • Personal information will only be shared with authorised coaches, welfare personnel and other relevant individuals where necessary.
  • Medical, emergency contact and safeguarding information will be handled confidentially.
  • We will take appropriate steps to protect children and young people from inappropriate use or disclosure of their information.

Parents and guardians may contact us with questions about the personal information collected about their child, subject to the child's own data protection rights and interests.

Our advertised rowing camps may have specific age eligibility requirements. Any activities involving under-18s will be subject to appropriate safeguarding, consent and information-handling arrangements.

7. Photographs and Video Recording

Photographs and video recordings may be taken during rowing camps, training sessions and organised activities for coaching, performance analysis, promotional and communication purposes.

We distinguish between coaching-related recordings and promotional photography.

Coaching and Performance Analysis

Images and recordings may be used for relevant coaching, technical feedback and performance development purposes. Access will be restricted to authorised individuals involved in the relevant coaching activity.

Promotional Photography and Video

Photographs and videos may be used for promotional purposes, including:

  • The Row-silience website.
  • Social media platforms.
  • Marketing materials and advertisements.
  • Promotional publications and business communications.

Promotional use will be subject to separate, optional permission.

Permission for promotional photography will not be a condition of participation in our camps, coaching sessions or training programmes.

For participants under 18, appropriate permission will be obtained from a parent or legal guardian, taking account of the young participant's own wishes and circumstances.

Participants, parents or guardians may withdraw promotional photography permission by contacting us. Withdrawal will apply to future use and will not affect processing that took place lawfully before withdrawal. We will take reasonable steps to stop future use of affected material, although we may not be able to retrieve material already published or shared by third parties.

8. Sharing Your Information

We will not sell or rent personal information to third parties.

We may share relevant personal information with the following categories of individuals and organisations where necessary:

  • Coaches and authorised programme staff: To organise and deliver coaching sessions, training programmes and camps.
  • Welfare and safeguarding personnel: To support participant welfare and safeguarding arrangements.
  • Medical professionals and emergency services: Where necessary to respond to medical incidents or protect someone's safety.
  • Venue operators and accommodation providers: Where information is necessary to organise and deliver a camp or other activity.
  • Form and website service providers: Including Tally, which we use to collect enquiries and registrations, and GitHub Pages, which hosts our website.
  • Domain and technical service providers: Where necessary to maintain the website and its associated services.
  • Insurance providers and professional advisers: Where necessary for insurance, legal, accounting or business administration purposes.
  • Governing bodies and regulatory organisations: Where information must be shared for legitimate regulatory, safeguarding or compliance purposes.
  • Relevant authorities: Where disclosure is required by law or necessary to protect an individual's vital interests.

Any information shared will be limited to what is reasonably necessary for the relevant purpose.

Third-party providers processing information on our behalf are expected to handle it in accordance with applicable data protection requirements.

9. Third-Party Services and International Data Transfers

Row-silience currently uses third-party services to support its website and business operations. These include:

  • Tally: Online enquiry and registration forms.
  • GitHub Pages: Website hosting.
  • Domain provider: Domain management and associated technical services.

These providers may process limited personal or technical information as part of providing their services.

Some providers may process or store information outside the United Kingdom. Where personal information is transferred internationally, we will ensure that appropriate safeguards are in place in accordance with applicable data protection legislation.

We encourage individuals to review the privacy information provided by third-party services when using their platforms.

We will update this policy if we introduce additional services, such as online payment processing, customer membership accounts, automated email services or website analytics.

10. Data Storage and Security

We take appropriate technical and organisational measures to protect personal information against unauthorised access, loss, misuse, alteration or disclosure.

These measures include:

  • Restricting access to personal information to authorised individuals.
  • Using appropriate account security and access controls.
  • Keeping electronic and physical records secure.
  • Limiting the collection of information to what is reasonably necessary.
  • Taking appropriate steps to securely delete information when it is no longer required.

Personal information may be stored electronically or in secure physical records.

No method of electronic storage or internet transmission is completely secure. However, we take reasonable steps to protect information in accordance with the sensitivity of the data being processed.

11. Data Retention

We retain personal information only for as long as reasonably necessary to fulfil the purposes for which it was collected, including applicable legal, accounting, insurance, safeguarding and regulatory requirements.

Our intended retention periods include:

  • General enquiries that do not lead to a service: Normally up to 12 months after the last meaningful contact.
  • Camp registration and attendance records: Normally up to three years following the relevant activity, unless a longer period is necessary for an incident, complaint, insurance matter or legal claim.
  • Medical and health information: Normally deleted when no longer required for safety, welfare or necessary follow-up, with a target of three months after the relevant activity unless an incident, claim or legal requirement requires longer retention.
  • Financial and accounting records: Retained for the period required by applicable tax and accounting legislation.
  • Photography and promotional consent records: Retained for as long as the relevant images are used and for an appropriate period afterwards to demonstrate the permission obtained.

Where information is no longer required, it will be securely deleted, destroyed or anonymised.

12. Cookies and Website Information

Row-silience does not currently use Google Analytics, advertising cookies or other website analytics and tracking tools.

Our website may involve strictly necessary technical processes required to operate, maintain and secure the website.

Some links on our website direct visitors to third-party services, including Tally forms. Those services may use their own cookies or similar technologies when visitors access their platforms.

Where we introduce non-essential cookies or similar technologies in the future, we will update this policy and obtain consent where legally required.

Please refer to our Cookie Policy for further information.

13. Your Data Protection Rights

Under applicable data protection legislation, individuals may have the right to:

  • Request access to the personal information we hold about them.
  • Request correction of inaccurate or incomplete information.
  • Request deletion of personal information where legally appropriate.
  • Request restriction of certain processing activities.
  • Object to certain uses of personal information.
  • Request transfer of information where the right to data portability applies.
  • Withdraw consent where processing is based on consent.

These rights are subject to certain legal conditions and exemptions.

To exercise any of these rights, please contact us using the details provided below.

We may need to verify your identity before responding to a request.
