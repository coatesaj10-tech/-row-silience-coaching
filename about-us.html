ABOUT ROW-SILIENCE

Good rowing is
built together.

Row-silience Coaching helps rowers take their next step through thoughtful coaching, personalised training plans and on-water camps. We work with individuals and clubs to build skills, confidence and performance around each rower’s goals.

Meet your coaches

  • Holly Dunford

    Holly began her rowing journey at the age of 12 at Molesey Boat Club before earning a scholarship to row at the University of Washington.

    Alongside her collegiate career, Holly continued to represent Great Britain internationally, achieving significant success at Under-23 level with a gold and silver medal respectively.

    Following her graduation, Holly returned to the UK to join the Great Britain Senior team. The pinnacle of her international career came at the Paris 2024 Olympic Games, where she won an Olympic bronze medal.

    Since the Games, Holly has continued to compete at the highest level, adding victory at Henley Royal Regatta to her achievements while developing her coaching expertise and passion for athlete development.

  • AJ Coates

    AJ began his rowing journey as a cox at St Peter’s School, York, aged 12, before transitioning to rowing himself. He continued his development at the University of York, where he served as Men’s Captain, taking responsibility for leading the programme, overseeing training plans, organising training camps and managing its day-to-day operations.

    Following graduation, AJ joined Molesey Boat Club, where he continues to compete. During his time there, he has reached multiple Henley Royal Regatta semi-finals and most recently represented Great Britain at the FISU World University Championships, securing a silver medal.

    Alongside his own competitive career, AJ is passionate about coaching and helping athletes develop their technical skills, physical performance and confidence on the water. He combines his racing experience with his leadership background to support athletes in achieving their goals.
