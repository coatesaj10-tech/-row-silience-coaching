MORE THAN MILES

What our camps
entail.

Three days of top-level coaching, provided by our elite coaches, Holly Dunford and AJ Coates.

    Session 1 · 08:00–09:30

    A steady UT2 session consisting of low-intensity miles.

    Session 2 · 10:30–12:00

    A shorter technical row focused on upskilling your rowing.

  • DAY 2
    Session 1 · 08:00–09:30

THE PEOPLE IN YOUR CORNER

Meet your
coaches.

Holly Dunford

Holly represented Great Britain in the Women’s 8+ at the Paris Olympics, winning a bronze medal. Since then, she has won Henley Royal Regatta with Molesey while continuing to develop her coaching expertise.

AJ Coates

AJ represented Great Britain in the Men’s 4− at the 2026 FISU Rowing Championships in Canada, winning a silver medal. He is keen to pass on his knowledge and expertise through coaching.
