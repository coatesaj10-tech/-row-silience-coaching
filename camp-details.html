MORE THAN MILES

What our camps
entail.

Three days of top-level coaching from Holly Dunford and AJ Coates, with personalised small-group coaching for a maximum of 10 people, for singles and doubles only.

£335 per person

  • DAY 1
    Session 1 · 08:00–09:30

    A steady UT2 session consisting of low-intensity miles.

    Session 2 · 10:30–12:00

    A shorter technical row focused on upskilling your rowing.

  • DAY 2
    Session 1 · 08:00–09:30

    A steady UT2 session.

    Session 2 · 10:30–12:00

    A shorter-mileage session with some HIIT, focusing on holding changes at rate.

  • DAY 3
    Session 1

    4–6 km pre-paddle.

    Session 2

    Performance pieces.

Also included

  • Video analysis between sessions to review technique and focus your coaching.
  • Post-camp feedback forms to reflect on your experience and guide your next steps.

THE PEOPLE IN YOUR CORNER

Meet your
coaches.

Holly Dunford

Holly represented Great Britain in the Women’s 8+ at the Paris Olympics, winning a bronze medal. Since then, she has won Henley Royal Regatta with Molesey while continuing to develop her coaching expertise.

AJ Coates

AJ represented Great Britain in the Men’s 4− at the 2026 FISU Rowing Championships in Canada, winning a silver medal. He is keen to pass on his knowledge and expertise through coaching.
